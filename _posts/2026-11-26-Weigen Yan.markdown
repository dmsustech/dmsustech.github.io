Denote the Laplacian characteristic polynomial of a simple graph $G$  of order $n$  and size $m$  by $det(xI_n-L(G)) = \sum^n (-1)^k c_k(G) x^{n-k}$. It is well known that $c_0(G) = 1$, $c_1(G) = 2m$, $c_{n-1}(G) = nt(G)$, and $c_n(G) = 0$, where $t(G)$ is the number of spanning trees of $G$. The famous Turán's theorem implies that if $G$ is $K_{r+1}$-free and  $G \neq T_r(n)$, then $c_1(G) < c_1(T_r(n))$, where $T_r(n)$ is the $r$-partite Turán graph of order $n$. By using the Maclaurin’s inequalities, we show that if $G$ is $K_{r+1}$-free and $G \neq T_r(n)$, then $c_i(G) < c_i(T_r(n))$ for $1 \leq i \leq n-1$, which extends the classic Turán's result.

This is joint work with Danyi Li, Tianlong Ma.
