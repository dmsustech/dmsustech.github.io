A graph $G$ is said to be
determined by the generalized spectrum (DGS), if for any graph $H$, whenever
$H$ and $G$ are cospectral and their complements are also cospectral, then $H$
is isomorphic to $G$. Previously, we show a graph $G$ is DGS provided $2^{-\lfloor
n/2\rfloor}\det W(G)$ is odd and square-free, where $W(G)$ denotes the
walk-matrix of $G$. In this talk, we show that the oddness assumption can actually
be removed, thereby confirming a two-decades conjecture of the second author.  Joint work with Quanyu Tang and Hao Zhang.
