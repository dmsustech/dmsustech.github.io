The Pons--Batle conjecture on tree-child networks was regarded as a specific and challenging problem in the phylogenetics community. We found an unexpected weighted symmetry for a class of three-row Young tableaux with walls and holes. This weighted symmetry implies the Fuchs--Yu identity, which together with the bijection of Chang, Fuchs, Liu, Wallner, and Yu leads to a simple proof of the Pons--Batle conjecture. We construct an involutive proof of this symmetry via introducing decorated binary networks as an intermediate structure.

This talk is based on joint work with Feihu Liu (NKU) and Jing Liu (SDU).
